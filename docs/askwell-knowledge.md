# Askwell: product guide

This is what the Askwell chat on Askwell's own website answers from.

## What Askwell is

Askwell is an AI support chat for any website. You add information about your product, paste one line of code on your site, and a chat button appears in the corner. Your visitors ask questions and get answers right away, any time of day.

The chat answers only from the information you give it. It does not make up facts about your product from the internet.

You can read every conversation your visitors have with the chat in your Askwell dashboard.

## How it works

1. **Add your knowledge.** Sign up, create a project, and upload files or paste text about your product. This is what the chat answers from.
2. **Paste one script tag.** Open the Install page in your dashboard and copy your snippet. Paste it into your website.
3. **Visitors get answers.** The chat button shows up in the corner of your site. Visitors click it, ask a question, and get an answer from your information. You can read every conversation in your dashboard.

There are two plans: Free and Pro.

- **Free** costs $0.
- **Pro** costs $19 per month.
- A project is one chat for one website. Each project has its own knowledge, its own snippet and its own conversations.
- The message limit counts visitor messages per project, per month. When a project reaches its limit, the chat tells visitors it has reached its monthly limit. It works again the next month, or right away if you upgrade to Pro.
- You can upgrade to Pro from the Billing page in your dashboard. You can manage or cancel your subscription there too. If you cancel, you keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.
- Customizing the chat (color, position, label, animation, header text, hiding the badge) is a Pro feature.

## What the chat can read

You can give the chat knowledge in two ways:

- **Upload a file:** PDF, Markdown (.md) or plain text (.txt). Each file can be up to 10 MB.
- **Paste text:** give it a title and paste the text in.

Files and pasted texts count toward the same limit: 3 per project on Free, 25 per project on Pro. You can delete a source at any time, and the chat stops using it.

Tips for good answers: use clear, up-to-date product information, such as an FAQ, pricing, shipping and return rules, or a product guide. PDFs need real text in them; a scanned image of a page can't be read.

## How to install

1. Sign up and create a project.
2. Open the Install page in your dashboard and copy your snippet. It is one line that looks like this:
   `<script src="https://.../widget.js" data-key="pk_..." async></script>`
3. Paste it just before `</body>` on every page where the chat should appear.
4. Reload your site. The chat button shows in the corner.

It works on any website where you can add a script tag: plain HTML, WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, or your own app. There is nothing to host and no code to write.

The chat runs in its own space on your page, so it doesn't change your site's look.

## What Askwell doesn't do

Askwell keeps things simple. These are not part of the product:

- **Word documents and spreadsheets:** you can't upload .docx, .xlsx or .csv files. Save them as PDF or paste the text instead.
- **Reading your website for you:** Askwell doesn't crawl or scrape your site. Add your information as files or pasted text.
- **Lead scoring:** it doesn't rate which visitors are likely to buy.
- **Live conversation view:** new conversations show up in your dashboard when you refresh the page.
- **Handing a chat to a person:** there's no live agent takeover. The chat always answers on its own.
- **Choosing the AI model:** Askwell uses one AI model for every chat.
- **Teams:** each account has one person. You can't invite teammates to the same account.

## Common questions

**How much is Pro?** Pro is $19 per month. It gives you 5 projects, 2,000 messages per month per project, 25 knowledge sources per project, full customization, and no "Powered by" badge.

**Is there a free plan?** Yes. Free is $0 and includes 1 project, 100 messages per month, and 3 knowledge sources.

**Do I need to know how to code?** No. You copy one line from the Install page and paste it into your site.

**What happens if the chat doesn't know the answer?** It answers only from your information. If the answer isn't there, it says it doesn't know instead of guessing.

**Can I change how the chat looks?** On Pro, yes: button color, position, label, open animation and header text, and you can hide the "Powered by" badge. On Free, the chat uses the default look.

**Where do I see what visitors asked?** In the Conversations page of your dashboard.
